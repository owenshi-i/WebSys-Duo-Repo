About Me

Hi! I'm Elisha Faith Alcazar, a 2nd year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Bicol University. Ever since I stepped into college, I knew I wanted to be more than just a student — I wanted to be part of something bigger. That drive led me to immerse myself in organizations, leadership, and everything that comes with it.

My Daily Life as a Student

As a 2nd year BSIT student, my classes have shifted to synchronous online learning. While face-to-face interactions are limited, my days are still packed — from coding in Java and C, to understanding networking, web systems, technical writing, and more. One of my personal favorites is Digital System Design (DSD), where we work with breadboards and IC chips. I also love debugging code — as frustrating as bugs can be, the satisfaction of finally making it work is unmatched!

My Role as an Organization Officer

My org journey started in 1st year when I joined three organizations. I was a Junior Councilor in the Ways and Means Committee of BUCS-CSC, handling IGPs, events, and procurement. I was also a Junior Councilor in the Internals Committee of BU CIRCUITS, managing event documentation. I was also a member of BUCS PCO.


Now in 2nd year, I was elected as CSC Representative and appointed as Business Manager by the Committee on Appointments. I've handled major events like the Freshmen Welcome Party, BUCS Anniversary, Scientista Pantry, and many more.

Hobbies & Interests

Outside of academics, I find joy in working with breadboards and IC chips in Digital System Design, writing and debugging code, and organizing events. Seeing students participate and enjoy something I helped create is one of the best feelings in the world.

"It doesn't matter if I trip and fall — as long as I stand up again and keep going."

My Goals & Dreams

My goal is simple but meaningful: to graduate. Not just with a diploma, but with experiences, memories, leadership skills, and a heart full of lessons. Every challenge I face — whether in academics, org work, or life — is just another step forward.
