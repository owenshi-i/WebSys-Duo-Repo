February 24, 2026 — Day 1

The Start of Something New

Today marks the start of my daily blog for our Web Systems project. This activity was assigned to us last Thursday, and we were instructed to work in pairs. I only managed to find my partner yesterday, so everything is starting to feel more real now.

Setting up GitHub, creating branches, and learning how commits work feels overwhelming at first — but it's also exciting. There's something motivating about building and managing a project properly, even if it's a lot to take in.

Today was also a busy day. We had a long quiz in Information Management that really tested what we've learned so far. After that, I went to the dean's office for a meeting with the council and the dean to discuss the upcoming events. Balancing academics and organization responsibilities isn't easy, but it's definitely helping me grow.

This blog will be my space to document my journey as a second-year BSIT student and organization officer. I'm excited (and a little nervous) to see where this journey takes me. 🚀

February 25, 2026 — Day 2

Adjustments and Challenges

Today is the anniversary of the EDSA People Power Revolution, so classes are suspended and shifted to asynchronous until Friday. Because of this, we had to move Litvimusta online after the president did not approve a face-to-face setup, even though the dean initially allowed it. I had to revise the guidelines again.

I’m handling the music competitions, and it’s been stressful. Most participants and judges prefer face-to-face vocals due to audio quality, and they want the event to push through ASAP. We offered excuse letters, but not everyone is willing to miss classes, and adjusting the academic schedule just for one category isn’t possible.

It’s challenging balancing everyone’s concerns, but I’m doing my best. I just hope everything turns out successful in the end.
