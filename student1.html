About Me

Hi! I'm Rowena O. Acabado, a 20-year-old second-year BSIT student at Bicol University. I was born on August 7, 2005, making me a Leo, and my Chinese zodiac sign is the Rooster. I have a mix of outgoing and introverted personality traits, which helps me adapt to different situations. We currently live in P-2 Upper Biogssacan, Guinobatan, Albay, where I enjoy spending time with family and pursuing my passions.

Family

I come from a loving family. My mother, Marife Acabado, and my father, Arvin Acabado, have always guided and supported me in everything I do. I am the eldest among my siblings, which include my brother Radim Acabado, my sister Cathlyn Acabado, and my youngest sister Nina Fatima Acabado. Spending time with them, sharing experiences, and learning from each other has shaped me into the person I am today.

Organization Role

I serve as the Deputy Secretary General in CIRCUITS (Circle of Unified Information Technology Students), an IT department organization. In this role, I assist the Secretary General by documenting meetings, managing correspondence, organizing events, keeping accurate records, and ensuring smooth communication among members. This position has strengthened my organizational, communication, and leadership skills.

Academics & Skills

I excel in subjects that allow me to express my ideas and reflect on real-world situations. I enjoy opportunities to showcase my thoughts, solve problems, and engage in critical thinking. My technical skills include programming in C and Java, and web development using HTML and CSS. These skills help me pursue projects both academically and personally.

Hobbies & Interests

Outside academics and organizational work, I enjoy cooking and experimenting with new recipes, coding and working on tech-related projects, playing volleyball with my brother and cousins, and travelling and going on joy rides. These activities allow me to balance work and leisure while learning new skills and staying energized.

"I balance my quiet focus with bold action, turning challenges into opportunities for growth."

Goals & Dreams

My academic and career goal is to graduate as a competent IT professional, constantly improving my technical skills while applying my knowledge to meaningful projects. Personally, I aim to grow as a leader and communicator, maintaining a healthy balance between work and life. Within CIRCUITS, I strive to support the organization’s mission, ensure smooth operations, and mentor fellow students to help them succeed in their own roles.
